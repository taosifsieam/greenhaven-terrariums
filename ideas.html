Ideas for your own little world

01

Pair a single upright branch with low moss to mimic a lone tree in a forest clearing.

02

Use two or three grain sizes of stone at the base — it reads more natural than one uniform layer.

03

Leave one side of the container emptier than the other; asymmetry feels more like a real landscape.

04

Choose a container with a narrow neck if you want a closed, self-watering micro-climate.

05

A small piece of bark or a single red stone can act as a focal point — don't overcrowd it.

06

Place your terrarium where you'll actually see it daily — a desk or bedside table, not a forgotten shelf.

07

Terrariums make thoughtful gifts for new apartments, exam success, recovery, or simply "thinking of you."
